Cod Steak Tomato Balti

Tomatoes are still in season? Then this is definitely a must cook dish to make the most of out of them.

This curry is really quick and easy using frozen cod steaks that are available in every supermarket freezer — which makes it very economical and I'm using common spices that you wont have any problem finding - probably already in you store cupboard!

By poaching cod directly in the spices and tomato sauce it stays beautifully moist and it takes about 10 minutes prep time and 20 minutes to cook. So it's like a night off for me :-)

What is not to love about that one pan cook? Add rice or roti & dinner is served!

This dish is so delicately spiced, so as not to over power the delicate fish. It tastes amazing! A perfect bed partner to basmati rice paired with fried red onions and cumin seeds — which is also easy to make! A perfect Home away!

Click the Method tab below to see a step-by-step guide on how to make Cod Steak Tomato Balti along with some pictures as a guide.

ingredients

Here's what you need :¬)

serves 4

For the Balti sauce

  • 4 cod steaks 400g to 600g
  • 400g fresh ripe vine tomatoes sliced
  • 1 Tbsp ghee
  • 1 large onion ½ finely sliced ½ diced
  • 3 cloves fresh garlic minced
  • I Tbsp fresh ginger grated
  • 1 fresh green chilli finger (hot) or bullet (mild) dependent on your heat preference

For the Spices

  • 1 tsp cumin ground
  • 1 tsp coriander roughly ground
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp sea salt ground
  • 1 tsp white pepper ground
  • 125ml vegetable stock - a stock cube is fine!
  • Handful of fresh coriander chopped

method

Saute all your aromatics. Build the sauce. Poach the fish.

This is going to be a breath of fresh air!

Still, I will go through each step and prepare everything in advance — we do not want "damn no fresh coriander" today do we please :-) We have definitely got this one!

Just take the time to get everything ready, it makes things so much easier.

Set your ingredients up ready to go:

Saute all your aromatics.

Onions 5 to 6 mins. Garlic, ginger & chilli 1 to 2 mins

  1. Heat ghee in a large non stick wok or Balti dish on a medium heat & have the following ready:
    • 1 Tbsp ghee
    • 1 large onion finely diced & sliced half of each
    • 3 cloves, fresh garlic minced
    • I Tbsp fresh ginger grated
    • 1 fresh green chilli finger (hot) or bullet (mild) dependent on your preference —sliced
  2. Add your onions cook until softened and golden
  1. Add your garlic, ginger & chillies — saute until the raw small has gone.

For the Balti sauce

Cook spices for 1 minute, add tomatoes & 1/2 tsp salt cook for 8 to 10 minutes

  1. Add in the spices below and stir fry into the onions then stir constantly so the spices toast & do not burn.
    • 1 tsp cumin ground
    • 1 tsp coriander ground
    • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  1. Now add in your tomatoes, stir through and add in salt
    • 400g fresh ripe vine tomatoes
    • 1/2 tsp salt
  1. Mash the tomatoes slightly with your spoon, or use a potato masher (quicker!) so they break down into a rich sauce
  1. Add 125ml water to your crumbled vegetable stock cube & pour it into your pan.
  2. simmer until nicely combined

De-ice the cod steaks

Now your sauce is ready, place the frozen cod steaks into a little water to remove the excess ice - just so we minimise the amount of water being added.

Poach the fish

Frozen fish 10 to 12 mins. Fresh fish 6 to 7 mins

Have the following ingredients ready:

  • 4 cod steaks 600g to 800g
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt ground
  • 1 tsp white pepper ground
  • Handful of fresh coriander — chopped
  1. Pat the fish dry to remove excess water
  2. Now nestle them into your tomato sauce
  1. Sprinkle over your garam masala, white pepper, salt if required and fresh coriander
    • garam masala
    • white pepper
    • salt - to taste
  1. cover the fish with sauce
  1. Cover and allow to cook until the fish is opaque and flaky — 65°C minimum if you own a thermometer

Serve immediately to enjoy this at its absolute best!

Serving suggestion

Seasoned Basmati rice tossed in cumin seeds & sliced fried red onion. Delicite, balanced and OMW devine!
