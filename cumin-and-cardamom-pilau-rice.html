Cumin and Cardamom Pilau Rice

Cumin & Cardamom Pilau rice is a wonderful fragrant rice with light aromats that will not fight or mask the delicate flavours of dishes that it is served with.

The cumin adds a warmth earthiness while the cardamom adds a sweet aromatic flavour. And cinnamon? Well, most of us are familliar with its rich, earthy, natural sweetness that is balanced by a gentle heat that is not overpowering.

Cassia cinnamon tends to bolder and spicier than the Ceylon variety which is a little more delicate and sweeter, so just be aware of this when out shopping.

Examples of dishes that really benefit are my Rogan Josh Chicken & Mushroom Curry or my Butter Chicken both of which are not fiercly hot but still quite spicy with subtle flavours that are easily hidden.

This is fantastic alternative to plain basmati or boiled rice.

The way we cook this ensures that we end up with light fluffy separate grains of rice.

Interesting fact! Cooking in a vegetable stock adds a slight colour and is used in place of plain salt and it is this method from which the Spanish Paella dish were born from! Ha! now you know :-)

ingredients

Here's what you need :¬)

serves 4

  • 250g Basmati rice
  • 1 Tbsp vegetable oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 4 green cardamom pods lightly crushed
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 2 whole cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 450ml hot water
  • 2 OXO vegetable stock cubes

method

This rice dish requires starch free rice, so it will need rinsing until the water runs completely clear. Wash it two to three times to achieve this then soak for 20 minutes.

Fry your spices in a oil or ghee add drained rice then your boiling stock and cook! Simples see :-).

Let's cook!

  1. Once your rice has been rinsed and soaked for 20 minutes drain completely and heat a medium sauce pan and add your oil or ghee
    • 250g Basmati rice washed soaked and drained
    • 1 Tbsp vegetable oil or ghee
  2. Once the oil is hot add all your spices
    • 1 tsp cumin seeds
    • 4 green cardamom pods lightly crushed
    • 1 small cinnamon stick
    • 2 whole cloves
    • 1 bay leaf
  3. Fry until they smell fragrant then add your drained rice and stir through.
  4. Place water into a microwave or kettle and bring to boiling.
    • 450ml hot water
  5. Crumble your stock cubes into the boiled water.
    • 2 OXO™ vegetable stock cubes
  6. Add your boiling stock to the saucepan
  7. Soon as the pan comes to a rapid boil cover and cook on the lowest setting for 10 minutes "Do not open the lid or disturb"
  8. Remove the pan from the heat and allow to rest for 5 minutes. remove the cinnamon and bay leaf then fluff with a fork and serve.
